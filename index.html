Three Pillars

Message Guard, Voice Guard, and Home Guard.

One family account protects the inbox, the phone, and the home without asking the senior to install or manage complex tools.

Pillar 1

Message Guard

Message Guard scans texts, links, and QR codes in real time to classify content as Safe, Suspicious, or Scam before the senior engages.

  • Incoming SMS, shared link, or QR scan enters the pipeline.
  • AI detects phishing patterns, urgency, impersonation, and manipulative language.
  • If flagged, the adult child gets a calm alert with the reason, risk level, and suggested action.
Dad, this is your bank. Scan this QR code now or your account will be locked.
Suspicious

Message Guard Alert: suspicious message detected. Alert sent to family.

Pillar 2

Voice Guard

Voice Guard analyzes forwarded voicemails and uploaded call clips for scam-intent phrasing, impersonation, and pressure tactics.

  • Voicemails are forwarded automatically or uploaded by the family.
  • Audio is transcribed, scored, and explained in plain language.
  • Families see whether the caller sounds safe, suspicious, or clearly fraudulent.

Pillar 3

Home Guard

Home Guard turns an existing home camera or repurposed smartphone into an on-device fall-detection sensor that alerts the family when something happens.

  • The adult child mounts one device in a hallway or bathroom entrance.
  • Fall detection runs locally, so non-event video stays in the home.
  • If a fall is detected, the family is notified quickly with context and an event clip.

Two Layers of Protection. One Seamless System.

Layer 1: Real-Time Detection

Every incoming message, call, and suspicious link is screened in 600 milliseconds, giving families an immediate first line of defense before pressure and confusion can build.

Layer 2: Deep Analysis

High-risk content is escalated to GPT 5.5-level analysis for deeper reasoning, clearer context, and stronger confidence before the user acts.

Together, these layers deliver both speed and certainty...

Privacy, Security, and Accountability

Sensitive scam-check content is processed with care, and personal message data is deleted within 24 hours to reduce unnecessary retention.

The system is grounded in verified, high-quality data, so families are not forced to trust vague or untraceable signals.

Ongoing bias mitigation helps reduce harmful skew and keeps protection more consistent across different users and situations.

And when high-stakes warnings are delivered, accountability remains visible: what was flagged, why it was flagged, and how the user should respond.
